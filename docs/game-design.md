# Game design

In 1926, Elias Blackthorn died during a midnight gathering. A century later, the player enters Blackthorn Manor to investigate the official accident account. Eleanor, Thomas and Clara remember different parts of the night.

## Investigation loop

Explore the Entrance Hall, Library, Study, Laboratory and Grand Ballroom. Investigation can unlock a Hidden Passage. Inspect named objects, operate available drawers or mechanisms and read earned evidence in the journal. Return through the Entrance Hall to move between wings.

Reconstruct a torn letter and calibrate a memory machine using discovered clues. Dragging has labeled selection/control alternatives. Wrong puzzle attempts are retryable. Connect pairs of collected evidence on the board to earn four deductions; moving cards or decorative threads does not establish a deduction.

Ask the available ghost natural-language questions, choose a tone and optionally present up to three earned clues. Trust and suspicion affect reactions and detail, but low trust cannot permanently block the mystery. Ghost dialogue cannot submit puzzles or grant evidence.

Review theory statements, attach relevant supporting evidence and submit a conclusion. Deterministic support rules yield Truth Revealed, Partial Truth or False Accusation. Restart clears investigation state after confirmation.

## Presentation and access

The gothic visual palette uses midnight blue, violet, antique gold and spectral cyan. Six room compositions, three ghost portraits, visible object states and readable story events support investigation. Sound starts off and is optional. Reduced motion and non-drag controls preserve the same required actions. Broad accessibility certification is not claimed; see [progress](progress.md).
